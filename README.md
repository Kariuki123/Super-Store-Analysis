# 📊 Super Store Analysis Dashboard

A comprehensive Power BI business intelligence solution designed to analyze Super Store sales performance, profitability, customer behavior, and operational efficiency. This project transforms raw retail data into actionable insights through interactive dashboards and data visualizations, enabling data-driven decision-making. 【1-c13283】

---

## 🚀 Project Overview

The Super Store Analysis Dashboard provides an end-to-end view of retail performance by tracking key business metrics across sales, profit, orders, products, customers, and regions.

Using Power BI, this dashboard helps stakeholders:

- Monitor overall business performance
- Identify high-performing products and categories
- Analyze regional sales trends
- Evaluate profitability across segments
- Understand customer purchasing behavior
- Support strategic and operational decision-making

The project leverages Power BI's data modeling, visualization, and analytical capabilities to present complex retail data in an intuitive and interactive format.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

The dashboard was developed to answer critical business questions such as:

- Which products generate the highest sales and profit?
- Which regions contribute most to revenue?
- How are sales performing over time?
- Which customer segments drive business growth?
- What categories and sub-categories perform best?
- Where are profit margins declining?
- What opportunities exist for business expansion?

---

## 📈 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The dashboard tracks important business metrics including:

- Total Sales

- Total Orders

- Regional Performance

- Profitable vs. loss-making products
